# Readable summaries for people and AI

Write `summary` as actual paragraphs separated by a blank line (`\n\n`). Prefer a concrete overview of one or two short sentences; add a short supporting paragraph only when it helps explain the contribution or a material qualification. The opening paragraph must stand alone: readers should understand the material without opening the original. Do not cram the whole summary into one paragraph or turn it into a rigid template of headings, icons and bullet points.

## Source cards

Read the original body, relevant comments, user notes and prepared media before summarizing. Apply the media-compilation workflow for images and video. Identify the central subject, then select the information a reader needs to understand its contribution.

- Product: what it is and what it does, then the main functions and real usage conditions.
- Tutorial: what task it teaches, then the essential approach and prerequisites; retain decisive steps, not every operation.
- Case or visual reference: what is shown, then the distinctive composition, technique or outcome supported by inspected media.
- Opinion or personal thought: the actual claim, then the reasoning and its scope. Separate the user's own notes from the author's opinion.
- Resource list: what resources are collected and when they help; do not invent a tested method from a list of links.

Keep the original emphasis. Specific examples belong in the summary only when they carry the main point. Preserve corrections or evidence gaps that change how the content can be used. Attribute unverified claims to the author. Missing audio, inaccessible frames or mismatched content cannot be filled in by inference; retain index-only status when appropriate.

Example:

Lieflat Charts 的一次图表配色与视觉更新。

在统一灰阶、字体、留白和细线的基础上增加三套配色，多图展示不同图表如何保持一致。作者另有补充：SVG 可以导出，但不保留原有动效。

## Topics

First state the specific problem or area covered. Then synthesize what the sources contribute together: shared approach, useful differences, or unresolved disagreements. Explain what the reader can learn; do not merely enumerate posts. A single source is preliminary material, not a broadly established method. Use sources assigned to this topic and respect index-only exclusions.

Example:

如何把数据组织成图表、报告或演示。

先确定读者需要理解的数据关系，再选择图形和阅读顺序。多篇材料展示了统一字体、配色和留白的做法，具体导出能力需要按版本确认。

## Domains

First describe the knowledge coverage. Then explain the tasks these accumulated topics can support and the current boundaries. A domain is a boundary of available knowledge, not proof that the model has acquired a tested skill. Synthesize only within the domain's topic ancestry; a shared cross-domain source alone does not justify importing unrelated topic methods.

Example:

关于界面布局、信息呈现和设计参考的资料积累。

内容涵盖组件、图表、页面层级和交互案例，可以帮助寻找参考、组织复杂信息，以及讨论界面方案。

## Before publication

Check every changed source, topic and domain:

1. Can a new reader tell what it is from the first paragraph alone?
2. Do the following paragraphs preserve the central information rather than generic praise or advice?
3. Can consequential claims be traced to read text or inspected media? Are disagreements and meaningful gaps retained?
4. Is the writing concise, natural and divided into readable paragraphs, without repetitive filler such as “赋能”“深度解析”“全面提升”?

Keep detailed takeaways, steps, checks, triggers, limitations and evidence in their structured fields. Give sources 2–5 specific keywords and knowledge nodes a few readable keywords; preserve manual tags. Human-readable prose does not replace provenance or AI retrieval metadata. Review retained relations, publish through preview/save and verify saved coverage and freshness. The browser detail view must render all summary paragraphs, even if map previews use only the lead.
